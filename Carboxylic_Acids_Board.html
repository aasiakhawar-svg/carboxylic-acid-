💡 Hint

In an exam, a displayed formula must show all the bonds in the –COOH group (C=O and O–H). In an equation, just write it as CH3COOH.

🔑 Key point: naming

Count all carbon atoms, including the one in –COOH. Propanoic acid has 3 carbons, hence "prop". The –COOH is always on the end carbon, so no number is needed in the name.

🎓 Extension: strong ≠ concentrated

Strong and weak describe how much an acid dissociates, not its concentration. HCl dissociates completely; only about 1 in 75 CH3COOH molecules form H⁺ and CH3COO⁻. So ethanoic acid reacts more slowly than HCl of the same concentration: the concentration of H⁺ is much lower.

⚖️ Writing the salt

Magnesium ethanoate is ionic: it contains Mg2+ and CH3COO⁻. You need two ethanoate ions for every Mg2+ to balance the charges, so it is (CH3COO)2Mg. The metal is written after the ethanoate.
Mg²⁺ (2+)+CH₃COO⁻+CH₃COO⁻= 0 overall ✔
Sodium reacts dangerously; zinc gives bubbles more slowly than with hydrochloric acid; metals below hydrogen in the reactivity series do not react.
